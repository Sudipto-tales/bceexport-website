About Us

Premium Handicrafts from India for the World

BCE Export proudly delivers premium Indian handicrafts to global markets, showcasing the rich heritage, artistry, and craftsmanship of skilled Indian artisans. We specialize in exporting high-quality Indian handicrafts including wooden items, glass products, handmade décor, photo frames, and traditional handicraft products. Each item is carefully selected to reflect authenticity, creativity, and exceptional quality. Our mission is to connect India’s traditional craftsmanship with international buyers who value unique handmade products and reliable sourcing. With strong logistics support, strict quality standards, and professional export management, we ensure timely delivery to our global partners, helping businesses worldwide access authentic Indian handicrafts with confidence.
